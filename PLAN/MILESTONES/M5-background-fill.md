# Milestone 5: Background-coloured coverage fill (`fill: background`)

Adds an option to fill the coverage the fill invents with **background** colour instead of foreground colour: where a defocused foreground scatters off its silhouette and the renderer wrote nothing behind it, `fill: background` synthesises the hidden sample the renderer omitted — borrowed from the nearest source pixel whose deepest surface lies behind the foreground — and lets the existing partition, scatter, holdout and composite treat it exactly as a `DeepMerge`-supplied occluded sample. The shipped `fill: foreground` behaviour stays bit-identical and remains the default. Small in UI surface (one dropdown, one radius knob); the substance is a bounded, depth-aware, band-invariant search. Runs before M2.

## Design (read before any task)

**The one mechanism: synthesise the hidden sample, then change nothing else.** M4's fill leaves a hole FG-coloured because the virtual background carries no colour. The halo rig (scene (m) m1: FG card at z=4, r=16 px; BG *on the focal plane* at z=20, r=0) shows the hole is **not** a pixel with `T > 0` — the FG is opaque (`T = 0`). The missing information is the BG sample the renderer would have written *behind* each FG pixel. The M4 control m0 already proves that when those samples are present (`deepMerge([haloForeground(), haloBackground()])`) the pipeline gives alpha 1 and the correct FG:BG mix in the halo band with no further help. So in `background` mode, for each non-empty source pixel P, the node appends one synthetic `deepc::SampleRecord` to P's sample list **before** `flattenPixelToSoA()` — a copy of a nearby pixel Q's deepest surface (its `zFront/zBack`, alpha, premultiplied channels) — and calls the unchanged flatten. Everything downstream is then already correct by M4's own pins: the partition gives it share `T_P·α_Q` (0 behind opaque FG, `T_P·α_Q` behind a semi-transparent one, consuming the residual so the pixel's residual becomes `T_P·(1−α_Q)` at Q's radius); the fragment scatter deposits its colour **and** alpha together, into the bucket of **its own depth** (behind the FG), with holdout visibility folded per deposit and the raw kernel row into `arrival`; the coverage-partition composite puts it behind the FG's claimed area; the deficit-only division and clamp behave as m0 measures. Fill and holdout commute because the synthetic is an ordinary fragment. This is Option A in its strongest form (the "BG deposit carries visibility like any other deposit"), and it rejects Option B (a separately scattered BG-estimate plane composited under the deficit): B needs its own holdout lookup per deposit, cannot show BG *through* a semi-transparent FG (no deficit there), and would add K-independent colour planes. **Independent oracle: the `DeepMerge` twin.** On a uniform BG the synthetic sample equals the real one exactly, so the BG-mode render of the sparse source is expected **bit-identical** to the twin's render (under the nearest-source estimator; within float noise under the averaging one).

**Where the BG colour is produced: a per-band surface map, two-pass fetch, `background` mode only.** Pass 1 walks `deepEngine()` rows over the band's fetch window **extended by the search reach** (`band ± (padY + reach)`, clipped to the output box; X is the full output box, so empties outside `srcBox` are simply absent) and records each pixel's *deepest staged surface* — the sample with the greatest sanitised `zBack` among `alpha > 0` samples, under exactly the flatten's rules (`sanitizeSampleDepth`, `rayDepthScaleAt`, `zb < zf → zb = zf`; α=0 samples are not surfaces, per the F1 ruling) — as `zFront, zBack, alpha, radiusPx, channels[C]` (premultiplied) plus a max-`zFront` tile pyramid over the map. No `SampleRecord` vectors are built in pass 1. Pass 2 is today's fetch+flatten loop with the synthesis inserted between `fillSampleRecords()` and `flattenPixelToSoA()`. The refetch is the price of BG mode and is measured at sign-off (rows are already fetched by ~2 overlapping bands each); `foreground` mode never runs pass 1, never allocates the map, and calls the flatten with an unchanged signature — zero cost, bit-identical by construction, pinned by a two-`.so` pixel diff. The map and pyramid are per-`BandJob` like `ResidualWindow` and are counted in `bandBudgetBytes()` (M4's rule: count it, don't name it as an omission).

**"Behind the FG surface", and why a plain depth tolerance is not enough.** Q qualifies for P iff `zFront_Q > zBack_P + max(kFillDepthTol · zBack_P, kFillSlope · g_P · d(P,Q))`. The first term rejects coplanar noise. The second is load-bearing: any purely relative tolerance makes a *receding continuous surface* borrow from a deeper part of itself — invisible when opaque (fully occluded, costs a fragment) but **visible on semi-transparent receding surfaces** (an α=0.9 ramp over nothing would read 0.99). `g_P` is P's own surface's local depth step, per axis the *smaller* of the two opposite-neighbour steps (so a silhouette pixel reads its interior step, not the jump to the BG; empty neighbours give no information), `g_P = max(gx, gy)`; a surface therefore never qualifies as its own background, while a real BG behind a card (`g_P ≈ 0`) qualifies at the first term. Constants start at `kFillDepthTol = 0.02`, `kFillSlope = 4`, fixed and documented, chosen and recorded against the m1/m3/near-card rigs in P2.T1. Post-check prune: if P is opaque (`T_P ≤ kFillDeficitTol`) **and** `radius_Q ≥ radius_P`, do not synthesise — a BG whose disc is at least as large as the FG's reaches the vacated band from outside the silhouette on its own (no deficit exists there), so the synthetic would only cost a fragment. A semi-transparent P always synthesises when Q exists (the BG must show through it).

**Empty pixels never borrow.** An empty pixel has no surface to be behind; borrowing would turn emptiness around an isolated element into an opaque field of the nearest object's colour and would extend a bbox-tight BG plane over the whole padded band. Empties keep `T = 1` at the `background_depth` radius, colourless, in both modes; scene (d) "empty exactly black" and the m2 bloom are pinned unchanged in BG mode. The alternative (BG-coloured blooms over emptiness) is declined for that reason and recorded below.

**The search: bounded, band-invariant, O(log N) when nothing qualifies.** Nearest qualifying pixel by Euclidean distance (ties by fixed scan order) via branch-and-bound over the max-`zFront` pyramid: a tile is pruned when its max ≤ the threshold at the tile's minimum distance (the threshold is monotone in `d`, so this is conservative), or when its minimum distance ≥ the best found. A BG pixel with nothing deeper anywhere prunes at the root. Two tiers, per the user's ruling: within `fill_search` the primary estimator; beyond it, the **fallback** is the nearest single qualifying pixel out to `max_radius`; beyond that, colourless (today's FG fill). `fill_search` is in **pixels**, proxy-scaled like `background_depth`, `0` = auto = `2·radius_P + 1` (the vacated band is ≤ `r_P` wide and needs BG samples out to `r_P + r_Q < 2·r_P` when a deficit exists at all), clamped to `max_radius`. Whether the primary estimator is *nearest-only* (`fill_search` is then simply the reach) or a *disc average* of all qualifying pixels within `fill_search` (a soft smear; the two tiers are then genuinely different) is decided from rendered pixels in P2.T3, per the user's standing preference — an in-focus textured BG behind a defocused card makes nearest-neighbour Voronoi streaks visible, and only a render can say whether they read worse than a flat smear. Band invariance: the primary and fallback reach are both ≤ `max_radius`, and pass 1 extends the map by exactly the reach, so every fetch-window pixel sees its full search disc whatever the band height.

**What must not change (all pinned):** `foreground` mode bit-identical to the M5-T0 `.so` (two-`.so` pixel diff, max ulps 0, on scene (m)'s kept EXRs and scene (a)); size-0 parity (a) bit-exact in both modes; holdout commutation — m4a/m4b in `foreground`, and in `background` the sparse halo rig under the 0.5 holdout equals the held `DeepMerge` twin; empties exactly black in both modes; fog identities (two 0.5 layers → 0.75) and a single α=0.5 card over nothing → 0.5 in both modes; the m3 α=0.9 ramp unchanged in BG mode (the slope rule's control).

**Cost model:** `foreground` — zero. `background` — pass 1 (~2.5× the fetch row-visits of FG mode), `O(N)` map+pyramid build, `O(log N)` search per non-empty pixel plus a disc scan only for pixels with a qualifying neighbour within `fill_search` (stride-capped so it never exceeds ~800 reads), and one extra fragment per pixel that synthesises. Measured at sign-off; the FG-mode profile must be unchanged within noise.

Constraints from the board apply in full: no SIMD, `DEEPC_HD` header-only kernels, `PodBuffer<T>` planes, doctest, 4-space/`_`member/lowerCamelCase, comment policy (no narrative comments, no plan/task references in code), every build and Nuke run through `scripts/hostguard.sh` (`--mem-gb 6` for the harness), work on `claude/deep-defocus-node-plan-o0ld83`. Validation stays green throughout — no task here licenses a red suite.

## Decisions

- 2026-09-11 — **User ruling: the BG colour source is a hybrid, user-tunable scheme** — a depth-aware search outward from the hole pixel for source pixels whose deepest sample lies behind the FG surface, taking that sample's colour and scattering the invented coverage at that sample's CoC, with a search-radius knob and a 2D-nearest fallback when nothing is found within it.
- 2026-09-11 — **User ruling: exposed as a `fill` dropdown `foreground | background`, default `foreground`**, today's shipped behaviour unchanged bit-for-bit; no legacy path and no extra blend slider. M5 is small and runs before M2.
- 2026-09-11 — **The BG colour enters as a synthesised hidden `SampleRecord` appended before the unchanged `flattenPixelToSoA()`, not as a new deposit path** (settles design questions 1 and 2): the M4 control m0 already proves the partition, scatter, holdout fold, bucket assignment by own depth and composite handle occluded samples correctly, so synthesising the sample the renderer omitted makes `background` mode equal to `foreground` mode on a `DeepMerge`-completed input — which is the independent oracle, bit-exact on a uniform BG.
